# Canva Browser Mastery

Use this skill when you design in Canva in a browser for Kind, including BLACK BOX social posts, MOTORLIST demo tiles and any other graphic. It covers the Canva web editor only. Kind forbids every Canva AI feature, so this playbook teaches manual design only, built from templates, elements, shapes, text and uploads.

## Design without Canva AI

- Never open Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Media, Dream Lab, Magic Edit, Magic Eraser, Magic Expand, Magic Grab, Magic Switch, Magic Resize, Magic Animate, Background Remover, Upscale or the Canva AI chat for Kind. Kind forbids all Canva AI generation.

- Build every design by hand from templates, elements, shapes, text and uploads. If a task seems to need an AI tool, stop and ask Kind instead of working around the ban.

- Do not use the Canva connector image generation tools for Kind either. The connector is fine for creating designs, uploading files and exporting, but its image generation and background removal tools stay off limits.

- Remove a background without AI by using a source image that already has a transparent background, or crop the image tightly and place it inside a frame.

- Resize a design without Magic Resize by creating a new canvas at the new size and copying the elements across, then fixing the layout by hand.

- Write copy yourself or reuse copy Kind approved. Do not ask Magic Write for headlines or captions.

- Generate images outside Canva only if Kind asks for that route. Never present an AI image as a manual design.

## Start a design

- Sign in at canva.com with Google and choose <YOUR_GOOGLE_ACCOUNT>. If Google shows a password screen, stop and let Kind enter it himself.

- Dismiss the Canva passkey prompt with Skip for now when it appears. It is a Canva account checkup page, not a required step.

- Click Create a design at the top right of the home page, then pick a preset or Custom size.

- Use presets when they match the destination. Instagram Post square is 1080 by 1080 px. Instagram Story and Reel are 1080 by 1920 px. Presentation is 16 by 9. A4 is 210 by 297 mm.

- Pick Custom size when no preset matches. Type the width and height and choose px, in, mm or cm. Canva accepts 40 to 8000 px per side and the total area must stay under 25,000,000 square px, so an 8000 px wide design can be at most 3125 px tall.

- Name the design at the top of the editor so it is findable later under Projects. Canva autosaves every change to the cloud.

## Read the editor

- Learn the three zones. The canvas sits in the centre. The left side panel holds the tools. The top toolbar changes with the selected element.

- Open Templates from the left panel to browse ready made layouts. Open Elements for shapes, lines, icons, stickers, charts, frames, photos and videos. Open Uploads for your own files. Open Text to add headings, subheadings and body text. Open Brand for brand colours, fonts and logos. Open Apps for extras such as Bulk Create.

- Read the top toolbar from left to right. File holds save, settings and print bleed. Undo and redo sit near the top left. Resize sits in the top bar and needs Pro. Share sits at the top right.

- Find finished and in progress designs under Projects in the left menu of the home page. Use folders once you have more than about 20 designs.

## Build from a template

- Choose the design type first so templates are filtered to the right dimensions. Then search the Templates tab with two or three keywords such as minimal product launch.

- Scan the first row of results before scrolling further. Canva relevance ranking usually puts a good match in the first five.

- Treat a small crown on a thumbnail as a Pro only template or element. You can load it on a free plan to preview, but the export carries a Canva Pro watermark unless you replace every Pro element.

- Change one element at a time and keep the template layout intact. Replace the text, swap the photo, then adjust colours to the brand. The spacing and font pairing in the template are what make the result look professional.

- Double click any text to edit it. Drag a new photo over an old one to swap it while keeping its position, size and crop.

- Save a finished layout as your own template with File then Save as template when you need the same format again. This needs Pro.

## Add and style text

- Press T to add a text box anywhere on the canvas. Or open Text in the left panel and pick Add a heading, Add a subheading or Add a little body text.

- Keep three levels at most. One headline, one subheading and one body style. Give the headline the biggest size and the boldest weight.

- Change font, size, colour, alignment, line spacing and letter spacing from the top toolbar with the text selected.

- Pair at most two fonts in one design. Use one display font for headlines and one readable font for body text. Keep the template fonts on a first pass because beginners usually pick worse pairings.

- Open Effects in the top toolbar with text selected to reach Drop, Hollow, Splice, Echo, Glitch, Neon and Curve. The current editor labels the shadow effect 'Drop', not 'Shadow', and there is no 'Lift' effect in the list (verified in the first browser build, 2026-11-06).

- Use Drop for depth and set its offset, direction, blur and transparency with the sliders — on the full desktop layout only. In the narrow layout the Effects panel offers the effect choice but no sliders.

- Use Hollow for an outline only look and set its thickness. Hollow works best with chunky fonts. Use Splice for an outline plus shadow cut out look.

- Use Echo for repeated shadow layers on short bold text. Use Glitch for a digital distortion look and pick one of its two colour pairs. Use Neon for a glow that works best on dark backgrounds.

- Use Curve at the bottom of the Effects panel under Shape to bend text into an arch or a circle. Drag the slider right for a downward arch and left for an upward smile shape. Curved text suits badges, seals and circular logos.

- Apply one effect per text box to solve one visual problem. Stacking effects muddies the text. For layered looks, duplicate the text box and style each copy separately.

- Keep body text readable over photos by adding a semi transparent rectangle behind it or by applying Lift or Shadow. Never place small text over a busy photo without a backing shape.

## Shapes, elements, frames and grids

- Press R for a rectangle, C for a circle and L for a line. Or open Elements and search for the shape, icon or graphic you need.

- Drag a corner handle to resize an element and keep its proportions. Drag the centre to move it. Press Delete to remove it.

- Recolour a shape from the top toolbar with it selected. Use the eyedropper in the colour picker to match a colour already in an uploaded image.

- Search Elements for frame to find shapes that crop photos. Drag a frame onto the canvas, then drag a photo onto it. Canva snaps the photo into the frame shape.

- Double click a photo inside a frame to reposition it or change its zoom without breaking the frame outline. This is the fastest way to make circular profile photos and shaped image crops.

- Use grids from Elements to build multi photo layouts. Drag one photo into each cell of the grid.

- Use charts from Elements for simple data graphics. Edit the values in the chart panel after placing one.

## Photos and uploads

- Open Uploads in the left panel and click Upload files, or drag a file from the computer straight onto the editor. Uploaded files stay in the account and are reusable in every later design.

- Search Photos in the left panel for free stock photos. Filter results to Free under Price to avoid Pro assets.

- Replace a photo by dragging the new one over the old one. Canva keeps the old position, size and crop.

- Keep uploads under the plan storage limit. Free plans include 5 GB. Check the file size before uploading large videos.

## Edit photos without AI

- Select a photo and click Edit in the top toolbar. The panel offers Adjust, Filters, Crop and Effects.

- Work in this order. Crop first, then fix light, then fix colour, then add detail.

- Start Adjust with Auto adjust for a quick fix, then fine tune brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, highlights, shadows and clarity with the sliders.

- Open Select area under Adjust to limit changes to All, Foreground, Background or a brushed area.

- Pick a filter and drag its Intensity slider down. Most filters look best at half strength or less.

- Use Effects for Duotone, Glitch, Pixelate, Vignette and similar treatments. Each has an intensity slider.

- Crop with the Crop button in the top toolbar and drag the handles, then click Done. Double click a photo inside a frame to adjust its crop instead.

- Do not use Upscale, Magic Eraser, Magic Edit or Background Remover for Kind. All four are AI tools and stay off limits even though they sit in the same Edit panel.

## Layers, position and alignment

- Treat every object as a layer in a stack. The top layer in the list is the frontmost object on the canvas.

- Open the Layers panel with Position in the top toolbar, then the Layers tab in the left panel. Click any row to select that exact element, which beats hunting for overlapping items on the canvas.

- Drag rows up or down in the Layers panel to reorder the stack. Or right click an element and pick Bring to Front, Bring Forward, Send Backward or Send to Back.

- Select several elements by holding Shift and clicking each one, or by dragging a selection box around them.

- Group a selection with Ctrl G so the pieces move and resize as one unit. Ungroup with Ctrl Shift G.

- Align a selection with Position then Arrange. Pick Left, Centre, Right, Top, Middle or Bottom. Select three or more objects and click Tidy Up to space them evenly.

- Watch for purple smart guides while dragging. They appear when an element lines up with another element or with the page centre.

- Lock a finished background or logo with Alt Shift L so later edits cannot move it by accident. Unlock it from the Layers panel.

- Change an element opacity with the Transparency button in the top toolbar. A semi transparent rectangle over a photo makes a clean backing for text.

- Type exact X and Y values under Position then Advanced when a drag is not precise enough.

## Colour and brand

- Open Brand in the left menu of the home page to set up a Brand Kit. Add logos, colours, fonts and templates there once and reuse them in every design.

- Paste HEX codes for brand colours instead of picking by eye. Keep the palette to 5 to 10 colours with 2 to 3 primary, 2 to 3 secondary and 2 to 3 neutrals.

- Upload logos as transparent PNG or SVG. Keep at least a full colour version, a white version for dark backgrounds and an icon only version.

- Set font roles in the Brand Kit for heading, subheading and body text. Pick from the Canva library or upload a licensed custom font on Pro.

- Know the free plan limit. A free Brand Kit stores 3 brand colours only. Logos, extra fonts and multiple kits need Pro, so on a free plan keep a written list of brand HEX codes and font names beside the skill and reapply them by hand.

- Do not invent brand colours. Use only the HEX codes Kind approved for that brand.

## Video and animation

- Start video from a video preset such as Video, Instagram Reel or YouTube Short. Reel and Story are 1080 by 1920 px.

- Add clips from Elements then Videos or from Uploads. Drag clips onto pages in order. Each page is one scene.

- Trim a clip by dragging its handles at the bottom of the editor. Split a clip at the playhead with Split in the top toolbar.

- Reorder clips by dragging them along the timeline at the bottom of the editor.

- Add a transition by clicking the plus icon between two clips and picking Add transition. Choose a style such as Fade or Slide and set its direction and duration. Use Apply between all pages for one consistent transition.

- Animate an element by selecting it and clicking Animate in the top toolbar. Pick Rise, Pan, Block, Pop, Breathe, Tumble or Drift and set speed, direction and easing.

- Animate a whole page by clicking empty canvas and picking a Page Animation. Do not use Magic Animate for Kind because it is an AI tool.

- Add music from Elements then Audio. Drag a track onto the page and trim it like a clip. Set volume and fade in or out from the audio toolbar.

- Set each photo page to 3 to 5 seconds for social clips. Export finished video as MP4.

## Download, share, publish and print

- Click Share at the top right, then Download. Pick the file type first, then the pages, size and quality.

- Export PNG for social posts and anything that needs a transparent background. Export JPG for photos and smaller files. Export PDF Standard for documents and online sharing. Export PDF Print for professional printing. Export MP4 for video and GIF for short looping animations. SVG needs Pro.

- Turn on Transparent background only for PNG, SVG or GIF. The checkbox appears after you pick one of those types. It needs Pro, Teams, Business or Education. Delete the page background first or set it to white.

- Use PNG at the highest size for Instagram posts and carousels. For a multi page carousel, export all pages as PNG in one download.

- Share a design with Share then Copy link. Choose View only, Can edit or Template link. A template link lets others copy the design without touching your original.

- Publish a design as a live website from Share then Publish as website. The page updates live when you edit the design. Canva gives it a Canva URL.

- Schedule social posts from Share with the Content Planner. It posts to Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X and TikTok at a set date and time.

- Order prints from Share then Print with Canva Print, or export PDF Print and take it to a print shop.

- Prepare print files with File then Settings then Show print bleed and add 3 mm of bleed. Keep important content inside the safe zone. Export PDF Print with the CMYK colour profile and Flatten PDF ticked for offset printing. Home or office printing can use PDF Standard with RGB.

## Collaborate

- Invite people from Share by email or link and set Can edit, Can view or Can comment. Owners control the design, editors can change it and viewers can only see it.

- Leave feedback with a comment. Select the element, press Ctrl Alt M and type the comment. Comments are visible to collaborators.

- Open Version history from File to restore an earlier version of a design.

- Organise shared work in folders under Projects. Set folder permissions separately from design permissions.

- Request approval before publishing on Teams plans. Approvers get an email link to approve or reject with feedback.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- Undo with Ctrl Z and redo with Ctrl Shift Z. Save on demand with Ctrl S even though Canva autosaves.

- Copy with Ctrl C, paste with Ctrl V and duplicate with Ctrl D. Cut with Ctrl X.

- Select all elements on a page with Ctrl A. Deselect with Esc.

- Add a text box with T, a rectangle with R, a circle with C, a line with L and a sticky note with S.

- Group with Ctrl G and ungroup with Ctrl Shift G. Bring forward with Ctrl right bracket and send backward with Ctrl left bracket.

- Copy a text style with Ctrl Alt C and paste it onto another element with Ctrl Alt V.

- Bold with Ctrl B, add a link with Ctrl K and make text uppercase with Ctrl Shift K.

- Grow the font with Ctrl Shift full stop and shrink it with Ctrl Shift comma.

- Lock an element with Alt Shift L. Tidy up spacing with Alt Shift T. Show rulers and guides with Shift R.

- Add a page with Ctrl Enter. Open Quick Actions with forward slash and search for any command by name.

- Zoom in with Ctrl plus, zoom out with Ctrl minus and return to 100 percent with Ctrl zero.

- Start a presentation full screen with Ctrl Alt P. On a Mac use Cmd in place of Ctrl and Option in place of Alt.

## Free versus Pro

- Stay on the free plan until a Pro feature earns its keep. Free covers templates, elements, photos, text, uploads, filters, basic video and 5 GB of storage.

- Treat the crown icon as the Pro marker. It appears on templates, elements, photos, fonts and features that need Pro.

- Pro adds Background Remover, Magic Resize, premium templates and stock, full Brand Kits, custom font upload, transparent background export, SVG export, Bulk Create, Save as template and higher AI limits. The AI items on that list stay off limits for Kind even on Pro.

- Pro costs about 13 to 15 US dollars a month, or about 120 to 144 US dollars a year. Check canva.com/pricing before quoting a price because Canva changes it.

- Education and Nonprofit plans include Pro features free for eligible users. Check eligibility with a school or work email.

## Browser driving notes

- Drive Canva in the live browser with browser.browse. Pass <YOUR_GOOGLE_ACCOUNT> as the account hint and sign in with Google. If Google shows a password screen, stop and let Kind enter it himself.

- Expect the Canva passkey prompt after sign in. Click Skip for now to reach the home page. The signed in account shows in the More menu.

- Use Custom size for exact dimensions. The Create a design dialog lists Instagram Post as 4 by 5, which is not the square 1080 by 1080 post. Type 1080 by 1080 in Custom size instead.

- Wait for side panels to load before clicking inside them. Templates, Elements and Photos load thumbnails over the network and a fast click can land on the wrong item while the list is still shifting.

- The editor can render in a narrow, mobile-style layout (bottom-sheet panels, bottom tab bar) and switch back to the full desktop layout on its own. Do not fight it — keep working; the same tools are there, just arranged differently.

- The Share dialog shows only the invite field and the owner, with no visible 'Copy link' row. Clicking the purple Share button copies the design link (toast 'Link copied to clipboard'), but the copied text cannot be read back — report the editor's edit URL as the verifiable link, and do not create a public view link unless Kind asks.

- Confirm every click by reading the new state. After picking a template, check the canvas changed. After typing text, check the words appear. Never assume a click worked.

- Prefer keyboard shortcuts for repeat actions because they are deterministic. Clicking small toolbar icons and drag handles is the least reliable part of driving Canva in a browser.

- Avoid drag operations that need pixel precision when a panel field exists instead. Use Position then Advanced to type exact X and Y values for placement.

- Uploads use the browser file picker. The browser task must be able to reach the file path. Keep source files under the workspace so the path is stable.

- Downloads land in the browser default download folder. Check the file exists and open it to confirm it exported as expected. Never trust a success message alone.

- Canva autosaves, so there is no save button to press. Confirm the save state in the top bar before closing a design.

- Some dialogs cannot be driven by clicking. The resize dialog on a free plan shows an upgrade prompt instead of resizing. Use the manual new canvas workaround from the resize bullet instead.

## CanvaLoop (Kind's process, set 2026-11-06)

- Kind rated the first BLACK BOX card 'not even 1/10'. A canvaloop is the fix process, and 6 canvaloops were ordered for that card.
- One canvaloop = (1) study award-winning and viral designs in the browser and analyse them, (2) harshly critique the current design against what was learned, (3) rebuild or improve it in Canva applying the lesson plus every lesson from earlier loops.
- Across the 6 loops, study at least 100 designs in total (about 17 per loop). Sources: Awwwards, Behance, Dribbble, Canva template gallery, and other top design showcases.
- Each loop extracts exactly ONE key technique, names it, and applies it in the rebuild. The lesson goes into this playbook under 'CanvaLoop lessons' with the loop number, the technique, and what changed in the design.
- Never skip the inspiration step and never rebuild from the same ideas that produced the weak version. If a loop's rebuild still scores low with Kind, the next loop studies harder, not faster.

## CanvaLoop lessons

- Loop 1 (2026-11-06): studied 24 designs (Awwwards 10, Behance 4 in detail, Dribbble 2 in detail, Canva templates). Technique: MAKE THE WORDS THE IMAGE — one enormous tightly-tracked wordmark as the single hero at near-full width, tiny microcopy only. Top 5 recurring techniques: (1) extreme palette restraint, (2) oversized wordmark as hero, (3) one focal object in vast space, (4) tactile material/glow on dark, (5) strict grid with small-caps microcopy and hairline rules. Applied to v2: BLACK BOX enlarged to ~90% width, spacing tightened, tagline shrunk to microcopy, hairline rule added, background deepened to #040404.
- Loop 1 build notes (v2, 2026-11-06): size wordmarks by width %, not pt — 116 pt spans 90% of a 1080 canvas (200 pt would be ~1690 px wide, off-canvas). A rectangle cannot be a 1–2 px hairline (5 px minimum height) — use a Line element with stroke weight 2. The R shortcut fails when focus is not on the canvas — add shapes from the Elements panel. In the narrow layout a font-size sheet can stick open — dismiss it by tapping the canvas. If a PNG download reports an unknown outcome, use Canva's 'download here' fallback link in its dialog.
- Loop 2 (2026-11-06): studied 24 NEW designs (Behance black branding, Dribbble chrome search, Awwwards winners, Pinterest chrome pins). Technique: ONE CHROME FOCAL DEVICE — a single small centred reflective object (the box itself, chrome/glass with a twist) at ~1/5 poster height, wordmark untouched. Top 5: (1) one chrome/reflective object centred small, (2) vast empty black as subject, (3) tone-on-tone depth (emboss, grey-on-black), (4) light as only accent, (5) living/modular focal device. Applied to v3: chrome box centred in the lower empty field below the tagline; sourced from Canva stock Photos/Elements only, never AI-generated.
- Loop 2 BLOCK (2026-11-06): no usable chrome box in Canva free stock — every reflective chrome/glass cube is Pro (would watermark the export), and the photo candidates sit on white backgrounds (white rectangle on a black poster). Closest: Elements 'Metallic, textured cube floating against a stark black background' (Pro). Design left unchanged as v2 per the fallback. Way forward: a free-licence chrome-cube photo from outside Canva stock (Unsplash/Pexels, black background), uploaded via Uploads — or a Pro upgrade, which is Kind's call.
- Loop 2 hunt result (2026-11-06): no clean solid chrome cube exists free on Unsplash or Pexels after extensive search (groups of cubes, liquid chrome, patterned cubes, non-black backgrounds). Closest found: a GLASS cube on pure black by Veli Batuhan Aytaç, Unsplash Licence, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-glass-cube-refracts-light-on-a-black-background-SsAOhMZ60Ao — single cube, reflective/glowing faces, thin rainbow dispersion edges. It is a 3D render, not a photo of metal. Saved locally as ~/workspace/canva-assets/glass-cube-black.png (converted from the WebP download). Using it for v3: glass counts under the loop-2 technique ('chrome or glass'); the rainbow fringes are thin edge-light, close to the 'light as only accent' technique.
- Loop 2 build notes (v3, 2026-11-06): the Position panel's ratio lock defaults UNLOCKED — lock it before resizing; a height-only resize squishes the image and can corrupt the frame (if it goes wrong, delete the image and re-add it, then resize proportionally). An object can sit off-centre inside its own image — centre the OBJECT, not the frame: measure the object's offset inside the frame and shift the frame to compensate. SEAM LESSON: a photo's pure black (#000000) against a #040404 background shows a faint rectangle edge around the image — the worker claimed 'no visible rectangle' but the seam is visible on the export. Fix: match the background to the photo's black (#000000) or crop the image tight to the object. Uploads file picker opened first time, no retry needed.
- Loop 3 (2026-11-06): studied 24 NEW designs (Behance minimal black poster grain, Dribbble black poster, Awwwards winners, Pinterest dark typography). Technique: EDGE-MICROCOPY FRAME — tiny small-caps labels at top left/right and a matching bottom row (left/centre/right), one tiny size, including a number such as 'n° 01', wordmark/cube untouched. Top 5: (1) top-and-bottom anchoring, empty middle, (2) edge microcopy as a frame, (3) grain/texture over the whole black field (also hides seams), (4) photo blended by matching its darkest tone to the field or bleeding to the edge, (5) one diagonal/vertical counterweight. Applied to v4: microcopy frame + background to pure black #000000 (seam fix); grain only if a free subtle texture is found quickly, never forced.
- Loop 3 build notes (v4, 2026-11-06): seam fix confirmed — pure black #000000 background makes the cube photo blend with no visible edge. The toolbar alignment button CYCLES Left–Centre–Right–Justify and does not reliably follow an inherited alignment after duplicating a text box — verify each label's alignment after every click ('VOL. 01' needed three clicks to land on Right). The editor flips between desktop and narrow layouts mid-task, staling references and closing panels — re-observe and re-select rather than trusting a stale reference. Grain search: 'grain texture' in Elements returns only Pro candidates (grunge graphics, 'Soft grain layer' photo, noise-grain videos) plus a third-party Texture app — no free plain grain element found. Duplicate-and-retype is the fast way to make a matched microcopy set: style one label, duplicate it, retype each.
- Loop 4 (2026-11-06): studied 24 NEW designs (Behance experimental poster diagonal, Behance free grain assets, Dribbble experimental poster, Awwwards 'Trippy Sites', Pinterest experimental diagonal). Technique: ONE DIAGONAL COUNTERWEIGHT — a single thin diagonal light streak (or the cube tilted a few degrees) running top-left to bottom-right behind the cube, cutting the horizontal stack. Top 5: (1) one diagonal/vertical cut, (2) oversized crop bleeding off an edge, (3) off-centre placement, (4) grain/noise as a unifying dissolving layer, (5) fragmentation of one letter/object. Supporting step: grain over the whole field — free packs verified on Behance Assets ('Dark Texture Pack 4K PNG', '20 FREE Subtle Grain Textures', '(FREE) 32 Film grain textures 4K') but downloads may need sign-in; a procedural noise PNG generated locally (PIL effect_noise, darkened) works as a free substitute, placed full-bleed behind all elements.
